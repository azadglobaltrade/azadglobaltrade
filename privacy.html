01

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when filling out our quote request form or contacting us via email, phone, or WhatsApp. This may include your name, company name, email address, phone number, destination country, product inquiries, and any trade references you choose to share while requesting a quotation.

02

How We Use It

Your information is used solely to respond to your enquiries, prepare accurate quotations, coordinate shipments, and comply with Indian export documentation requirements. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

03

Sharing & Disclosure

Details are shared only where strictly necessary to fulfil your order — with our freight forwarders, customs house agents, notified laboratories, and your nominated bank. We may also disclose information when required by law, court order, or competent government authority.

04

Data Security

We employ industry-standard measures to protect your personal data against loss, misuse, and unauthorised access. Access is limited to authorised personnel handling your export orders, and records are retained only as long as required under applicable trade and tax regulations.

05

Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and basic analytics to improve user experience — pages visited, approximate region, and device type. No personally identifiable information is tracked, and you can disable cookies in your browser at any time without losing access to our content.

06

Your Rights

You may request to access, correct, update, or delete your personal information at any time. We respond to all verified requests within 30 days.

To exercise your rights, email us at azadglobaltrade@gmail.com with the subject line “Data Request”.
