About Azad Global Trade

INDIA'S FINEST,
WORLD'S TRUSTED

Azad Global Trade is a leading export-import company based in India, specializing in agricultural products, traditional handicrafts, and sustainable eco-friendly goods. We bridge India's incredible diversity of produce and craftsmanship with discerning global markets.

Our network spans 200+ farmer cooperatives and artisan clusters across 12 Indian states. By eliminating middlemen, we ensure fair prices for producers and competitive pricing for buyers without ever compromising on quality.

Every shipment undergoes multi-stage quality inspection in our processing facilities, backed by complete documentation and certifications required by destination countries.

Our Mission

To make India's premium products accessible to global markets with transparency, quality, and fairness.

Our Vision

To be India's most trusted export partner - recognized for quality, reliability, and sustainable practices.
