Terms of Trade

Last updated:

1. Quotations

All quotations are valid for 30 days from the date of issue unless otherwise stated. Prices are subject to change based on market conditions, currency fluctuations, and shipping costs.

2. Orders & Acceptance

An order becomes binding only upon written confirmation by Azad Global Trade, accompanied by an official Proforma Invoice. Acceptance is subject to product availability and compliance verification.

3. Payment Terms

Standard terms: 30% advance upon order confirmation, 70% balance against shipping documents. Letter of Credit (LC) accepted for orders above USD 25,000. All bank charges are borne by the buyer.

4. Shipping & Delivery

Delivery timelines are estimates and not guaranteed. Risk passes to the buyer once goods are loaded onto the carrier (FOB terms, unless otherwise agreed in writing). Force majeure events release both parties from contractual obligations.

5. Quality & Inspection

Pre-shipment inspection reports are provided. Buyer must inspect goods within 14 days of receipt. Claims for defects must be raised in writing with supporting documentation.

6. Documentation

Standard export documentation includes Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary Certificate, Lab Test Report, Bill of Lading, Packing List, and Commercial Invoice. Additional documents available on request.

7. Disputes & Jurisdiction

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Any disputes will be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of courts in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India.
